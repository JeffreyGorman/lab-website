Join the Team

We're looking to expand the team, and welcome applicants with backgrounds in:

  • Synthetic organic chemistry
  • Biosciences
  • Materials chemistry / spectroscopy

We're especially keen to support cross-disciplinary training between these areas — if your background doesn't fit neatly into one category, get in touch anyway.

PhD Studentships

Externally-funded PhD positions are advertised online as they become available.

If you're interested in joining but don't see an open studentship listed, other funding routes worth exploring include:

  • SCOPE / EPSRC Doctoral Landscape Award (DLA) studentships (opens in a new tab)
  • Chinese Scholarship Council (CSC) scholarships (opens in a new tab)

Get in touch to discuss a potential project and funding route.

Postdoctoral Fellowships

Grant-funded postdoctoral positions are advertised online as they become available.

We're also very happy to support strong candidates applying for their own individual fellowship funding, including:

  • Royal Commission for the Exhibition of 1851 Research Fellowship (opens in a new tab)
  • Leverhulme Early Career Fellowship (opens in a new tab)
  • Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (opens in a new tab)
  • Newton International Fellowship (opens in a new tab)
  • EMBO Fellowship (opens in a new tab)
  • Career Development Fellowship (opens in a new tab)
  • Daphne Jackson Fellowship (opens in a new tab) (for researchers returning after a career break)

Get in touch as early as possible before a funding deadline so we have time to develop a strong proposal together.

Undergraduate Summer Internships

A limited number of paid summer research placements are available each year for undergraduate students, including via the RSC Undergraduate Research Bursary (opens in a new tab) scheme.

Places are competitive and limited, so please get in touch well ahead of any application deadlines.
